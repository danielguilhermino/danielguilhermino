### 👋 Hi, I'm Daniel

I'm a software engineer (building since 2014) who helps companies and founders **go from idea to software running in production**: backend, web app and mobile app, one person, one point of contact.

I like real business problems: running operations, money moving in and out, and automating the repetitive work that eats your team's week.

| | Service | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| 🧾 | **ERPs & internal tools** | Python backends, admin panels and mobile apps tailored to how your operation actually runs |
| 💳 | **Payments & fintech** | Payment engines, gateway integrations, reconciliation and financial dashboards |
| 🤖 | **Applied AI & automation** | AI agents, MCP servers and workflows that plug into your existing systems |
